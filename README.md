TruTag – Smart Food Safety Inspection & Verification Platform

Making Food Safety Inspections Smarter, Faster, and More Transparent.

TruTag is a digital food safety inspection and verification platform designed to modernize traditional government inspection workflows. It aims to reduce paperwork, streamline report processing, improve administrative monitoring, and make food business verification more accessible through technology.

By combining AI-assisted analysis, QR-based verification, digital inspection management, and centralized administrative dashboards, TruTag bridges the gap between field-level inspections and government-level decision-making.

Problem Statement

Traditional food safety inspection systems often depend on manual documentation, physical report submissions, and time-consuming administrative verification.

This creates several challenges:

Delays in processing inspection reports.

Accumulation of pending paperwork.

Limited real-time visibility into inspection activities.

Difficulty tracking inspection progress and compliance.

Lack of a centralized digital verification mechanism.

TruTag addresses these challenges by bringing the inspection and monitoring process into one connected digital ecosystem.

Key Features

1. Digital Inspection Management

Digitized inspection workflow for food safety officers.

Organized recording and submission of inspection information.

Reduced dependence on manual paperwork.

2. AI-Assisted Report Processing

Integration with Google's Gemini API for AI-assisted information analysis.

Helps streamline the processing of inspection-related information.

Supports faster administrative review.

3. QR-Based Verification

QR-based access to relevant food business information.

Simplifies the verification process.

Connects digital records with accessible verification mechanisms.

4. Centralized Admin Dashboard

Centralized monitoring of inspection activities.

Overview of submitted reports and pending cases.

Improved administrative visibility and management.

5. Role-Based Authentication

Separate administrator and inspection officer access.

Role-specific dashboards and workflows.

Controlled access to platform functionalities.
